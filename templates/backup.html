Game Rules

  • The game is played on a 5x5 grid.
  • Players take turns placing their mark (X or O) in an empty cell.
  • The first player to align 4 marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins!
  • If all cells are filled and no player has 4 in a row, the game results in a draw.
  • You can play the game against a friend or challenge the computer!(agnAp game engine)
